## What the resolution says

The executed resolution records adoption on June 2, 2025 and bears Board Chair Danny Knight's signature. It authorized **up to $50 million**, approved Scout Investments through its Reams Asset Management division as manager, allowed several implementation vehicles, and authorized staff to proceed if acceptable terms were reached.

The December funding notice completes the next step by showing money transferred to the manager account. Security-level account reporting would carry the public trail from authorization and funding through the assets held for members.
