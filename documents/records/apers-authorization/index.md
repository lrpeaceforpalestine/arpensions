## What the minutes say

Page 1 identifies Jason Brady as the State Auditor's proxy on the Board and Investment Finance Subcommittee. Page 4 records Brady presenting the proposal and moving to authorize APERS staff to invest **$25&ndash;$50 million**. Jim Hudson seconded the motion, which carried without dissent. The page bears the subcommittee chair's and executive director's signatures.

The minutes show how much the subcommittee authorized. The later transaction record shows what APERS actually purchased.
