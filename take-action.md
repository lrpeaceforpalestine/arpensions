## Sign on in support {#sign-on}

Add your name if you believe Arkansas pension members should be able to see the financial work behind covered investment decisions.

The petition asks the General Assembly to pass the **[Pension Investment Integrity Act](/legislators/)**. The proposal would require a written credit analysis, comparison with reasonable alternatives, liquidity disclosure, fiduciary determination, and public posting after a covered purchase of non-tradable sovereign debt. Trustees would keep the final investment decision.

## Contact your legislators {#contact-legislators}

Find your state representative and senator at [arkleg.state.ar.us](https://www.arkleg.state.ar.us/Legislators/), or use a constituent-contact service you already trust. The Legislature's page links to an external District Finder; an address entered there goes to that service, not to Arkansans for Pension Integrity. See our [privacy page](/privacy/) for details.

Three points are enough:

1. Arkansas pension members deserve a written financial explanation for covered purchases of non-tradable sovereign debt.
2. The proposal is issuer-neutral and leaves every investment outcome with trustees.
3. Citizens First Congress selected pension investment integrity as a short-term priority for 2027.

**Letter template.** Copy, personalize, and send:

<div class="letter-template letter-template--compact" markdown="1">
<button class="copy-letter-btn" type="button" aria-label="Copy letter to clipboard">Copy</button>

Dear [Representative/Senator],

I am writing to ask you to support the Pension Investment Integrity Act in the 2027 legislative session.

Arkansas pension boards have committed public retirement money to Israel Bonds, a form of sovereign debt that cannot be sold on a secondary market. APERS completed a $15 million purchase, and ATRS moved $50 million into a manager account for the strategy. Pension members should be able to see how trustees compared credit risk, expected return, liquidity, and reasonable alternatives.

The Integrity Act would create an issuer-neutral written process for future covered purchases while leaving the investment decision with trustees. Please support the bill and transparent, financially sound pension governance.

Sincerely,<br>
[Your name]
[Your city]

## Attend a board meeting {#attend-meeting}

Pension board meetings are open to the public. Attend to hear how investment decisions are presented, discussed, and recorded.

<div class="meeting-grid" markdown="1">
<div class="meeting-info" markdown="1">

**ATRS Board Meetings**

- **Location:** ATRS Board Room, 1400 W. Third St., Little Rock
- **Schedule:** Check the [ATRS calendar](https://www.artrs.gov/calendar)
- **Public comment:** Contact ATRS ahead of the meeting for current sign-up procedures

</div>
<div class="meeting-info" markdown="1">

**APERS Board Meetings**

- **Location:** APERS Office, 124 W. Capitol Ave., Little Rock
- **Schedule:** Check [apers.org](https://apers.org/) for current meeting information
- **Public comment:** Contact the Executive Director's office for current sign-up procedures

</div>
</div>

If you attend, listen for four things: credit risk, expected return, liquidity, and alternatives. Those are the building blocks of a useful public investment record.
